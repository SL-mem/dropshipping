Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship from warehouses inside the EU and typically arrive in 2–6 business days in the Netherlands and Belgium. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

How do I pick the right size?

Our outerwear is cut to fit over a sweater. If you're between sizes and plan to layer heavily, size up.

What is your return policy?

You can return unworn items with tags attached within 14 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Return shipping is paid by you unless the item is faulty or wrong.

Will you sell more than winter clothes?

Yes. Fourfold rotates its featured collection each season — spring, summer and fall collections are on the way. Join the list below to hear first.
