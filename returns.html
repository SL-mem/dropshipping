Returns and right of withdrawal

You have 14 days to change your mind. Here is how to return an item and get your money back.

Your right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The withdrawal period ends 14 days after the day you, or a person you named (other than the carrier), receive the goods. If you ordered several items that arrive separately, the period starts when you receive the last item.

To withdraw, tell us clearly before the period ends, for example by email to or by letter to . You may use the model form below, but you don't have to.

How to return

  1. Email us your order number and the items you want to return. We reply with the return address.
  2. Send the items back within 14 days of telling us. Pack them carefully, unworn, with labels attached.
  3. You pay the direct cost of returning the goods, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item.

Your refund

We refund all payments we received from you, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of the day you told us you are withdrawing. We use the same payment method you used, at no cost to you. We may wait with the refund until we have received the goods back, or until you show proof that you sent them, whichever comes first.

You may handle the items as you would in a shop to check them. If an item is worn, washed or damaged beyond that, we may deduct the loss in value from the refund.

Items that cannot be returned once opened

For hygiene reasons, socks and base layers that were delivered sealed cannot be returned once the seal has been removed.

Faulty or wrong item?

Email us a photo within two months of noticing the problem. We cover the return cost and repair, replace or refund the item under the legal guarantee.
